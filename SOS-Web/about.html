About The Label

A modern label built around artist control.

Your music. Your voice. Your choice.

SpacedOut Studios is an independent record label building a more direct, artist-first system for releases, roster identity, promotion, and long-term creative momentum. The goal is simple: give serious artists real structure without stripping away the control that made their work matter in the first place.

Artist-First Splits Our model is centered around artists keeping the majority of their royalties.
Real Infrastructure Registered LLC with BMI, ASCAP, TooLost, and SubmitHub systems in place.
Selective Roster Growth We use applications and direct invitations to keep the label focused and intentional.

What We Do

Artist Development

We help artists sharpen the world around their music: identity, presentation, sound, visual direction, positioning, and the story listeners understand when they first encounter the artist.

What We Do

Release Support

We are building a cleaner release pipeline for metadata, assets, artist pages, review, and communication, so releases are handled with more clarity and less chaos.

What We Do

Promotion & Presence

A release needs more than distribution. We focus on the public-facing layer too: social presence, playlist strategy, brand consistency, artist pages, and visibility.
