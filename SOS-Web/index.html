About

Why Spaced Out?

Let's face it. The music industry today is convoluted and some labels are downright thieves. Here, we are different. We aim to support and push our artists to the next level.

Identity

We are our artists. We stand by their sound, their innovation a dedication to their craft.

Consistency

Consistency is a key factor for success wherever you go. That's why are Spaced Out, we hold ourselves to a standard higher than what is already present in the industry today.

Presence

From the metal guitars and dubstep of 4nzek, to the funky basslines mixed with chill vibes from Chroma Glow, we strive to be seen. Not only by curating a group of creators who are truly unique but by supporting their visibility in an oversaturated market

Growth

Being a Spaced Out artist is more than being part of a unique family of creators. We help eachother grow with Meta Ads, pitching, cross-posting and so much more.
