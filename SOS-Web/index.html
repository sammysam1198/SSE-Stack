Why SpacedOut

A label should create momentum.

The music industry is crowded, confusing, and often unfair to artists. We are building a label system that treats releases, branding, promotion, and artist identity as one connected process.

Identity

We care about the world around the artist: sound, visuals, message, presence, and how it all connects.

Consistency

Strong releases need structure. We are building repeatable workflows for applications, roster pages, release assets, and review.

Visibility

Artists need more than a upload button. We support discovery through presentation, social presence, playlist strategy, and promotion.

Control

High artist splits matter. Our model is built around keeping the artist in the strongest possible position.
