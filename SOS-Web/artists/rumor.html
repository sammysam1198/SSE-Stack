About

Rumour

Rumour is a singer and songwriter from Waldorf, Maryland whose journey into music began early, winning his first singing competition at just 17. Introduced to guitar by his father, he quickly developed a deeper connection to music that would expand far beyond performance.

Driven by curiosity and dedication, Rumour taught himself production and sound engineering while continuing to write and compose his own material. Years of experimenting across multiple genres and collaborating with local artists have shaped a versatile sound and a clear artistic voice.

Inspired by the tonal worlds of artists like The Weeknd and The Chainsmokers, Rumour blends pop, R&B, and EDM with ambient vocal textures to create something distinctly his own. With a focus on standing out and pushing creative boundaries, his music is built to resonate and inspire.
