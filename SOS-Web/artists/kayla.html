About

Kayla Jay

Kayla Jay is a singer, songwriter, and producer whose sound is shaped by a life of constant movement and cultural exposure. Raised in a military family, she spent her formative years relocating across the United States and abroad, with time in places like North Carolina, Maryland, and Spain all contributing to her evolving artistic identity. Now based in New Jersey, her music reflects a broad perspective and a deep sense of self-built through experience.

Blending elements of pop and R&B, Kayla Jay delivers a sound that is both emotionally resonant and widely accessible. Her work is defined by strong songwriting, expressive delivery, and a natural charisma that draws listeners in.

With a growing audience and a clear artistic vision, Kayla Jay is positioning herself as a standout emerging voice, bringing both depth and presence to every release.
