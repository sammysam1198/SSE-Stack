About

Madgas

Madgas, born Jaques Malgas, is a 20-year-old rapper and songwriter emerging from Oudtshoorn, South Africa. Raised in a challenging environment, he has transformed adversity into creative fuel, channeling his experiences into deeply personal and emotionally charged music.

> Through sharp lyricism and introspective storytelling, Madgas explores themes of pain, loss, determination, and personal growth. His work reflects not only where he comes from, but the mindset driving him forward; One rooted in self-awareness, resilience, and unshakable hunger to rise beyond his circumstances.

With a sound grounded in authenticity and a voice shaped by real-life experience, Madgas represents a new generation of artists turning struggle into purpose and expression into momentum.
